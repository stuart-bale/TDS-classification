What this PCA contains

This is a pooled spectral-only PCA, not the combined waveform/spectral fit. Features are log peak frequency, fractional FWHM, power fraction inside FWHM, and peak/background contrast in V2, V1−V2 and V3−V4. These channels occur in both nominal configurations. V4/V5 and SCM measurements are retained in the downloadable channel catalog but are not inputs to this common-channel fit.

The 150 kHz power-supply harmonic series is masked before peak reselection, with guards covering the Welch main lobe. All three input channels must have a measurable uncensored width and peak/background ratio at least 3; saturated bursts are excluded. No missing-value imputation or validity flags enter the PCA. Narrow unresolved widths remain window-limited estimates. Three Ward groups are a browsing choice and are not physical classifications.

Welch: 4096 samples, Hann window, 50% overlap; bin spacing 468.75 Hz. Spectra use engineering units without frequency-response corrections. Peaks touching a harmonic mask or the search-band edge have censored widths and do not enter the PCA.
