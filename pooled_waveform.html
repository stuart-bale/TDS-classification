Analysis details

Seven shape descriptors per voltage channel: crest factor, kurtosis, signed skew-like asymmetry, 5–95% energy duration, energy asymmetry, envelope occupancy, and concentration of energy in the largest 1% of samples. Each of the three channel pairs contributes absolute sample correlation and envelope correlation. Common channels are V2, V1−V2 and V3−V4. These are descriptors computed from native waveforms; PCA is not applied to time-aligned raw samples. Waveform shapes can still contain instrumental signals.

PC axes are newly fitted and cannot be compared numerically with the old E23 axes. Group numbers are exploratory browsing labels. The separate spectral PCA contains the harmonic-masked frequency and bandwidth measurements.
